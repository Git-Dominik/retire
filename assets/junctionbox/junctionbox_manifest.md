# MSH_Industrial_JunctionBox_A

Built to DOORS_ASSET_GUIDE v20 (Mines biome, all parts crisp, no soft parts, no hand-made wonk).
Wall junction box: single-door enclosure, latch, hinges, rating plate, two clamped conduits to the floor, two sheathed cables with black boots rising into the wall.
Size about 2.6 x 7.7 x 1.0 studs, pivot on the floor at the wall, box centre (back face on the wall plane). Front is -Z in Roblox. 888 triangles in total.
