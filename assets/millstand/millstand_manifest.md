# MSH_Industrial_MillStand_A

Built to DOORS_ASSET_GUIDE v20 (Mines biome, all parts crisp, no soft parts, no hand-made wonk).
Two-high rolling mill stand: window housings on soleplates, grooved work rolls in chocks, crossbeam with screwdowns and a hazard-yellow handwheel, twin spindles to a gearbox and motor.
Size about 4.3 x 9.1 x 17.9 studs, pivot on the floor under the roll centre. Front is -Z in Roblox. 2,056 triangles in total.
